THE CLASSROOM

Where Every Movement Has Meaning

Our classes go beyond learning steps. Students are guided through technique, rhythm, expression and discipline, developing a deeper understanding of Indian classical dance.

TRAINING

What You Will Learn

Every class builds a strong foundation while gradually developing confidence, musicality and artistic expression.

01

Adavus & Technique

Learn foundational movements, posture, footwork, body alignment and controlled execution.

02

Mudras

Discover the language of hand gestures and learn how traditional mudras communicate ideas and stories.

03

Rhythm & Tala

Develop an understanding of beats, tempo, tala and the relationship between movement and music.

04

Abhinaya

Explore facial expression, emotion and storytelling through the expressive language of Indian dance.

05

Nritta & Nritya

Understand the difference between pure movement and expressive dance and learn how they work together.

06

Dance Theory

Gain knowledge of Indian dance traditions, terminology, history and the cultural meaning behind the art form.
